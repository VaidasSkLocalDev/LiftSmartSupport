Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains what data the LiftSmart apps for iOS and Android ("LiftSmart", "we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect what is needed to run your training, we do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and you can delete everything at any time from inside the app.

1. Data we collect

Account data

  • Registered accounts (Sign in with Apple or Google on iOS, Google on Android): your email address and display name as provided by the sign-in provider, account creation and last-login timestamps, and how the account authenticates.
  • Guest accounts: you can use LiftSmart without registering. A guest account is an anonymous identifier with no email or name attached. If you later register, your training data moves to the registered account.

Profile data you provide

  • Bodyweight and your preferred weight unit (kg/lb) — used to log bodyweight exercises and prescribe loads.
  • Gender (optional) — used to tailor which programs the app shows you and, while you share usage data, in analytics.
  • Training preferences from onboarding and the program questionnaire: goal, experience level, schedule, equipment and muscle focus.
  • Profile photo (optional, registered accounts only) — a photo you choose with the system photo picker. The app receives only that photo, never your library. It stores a small square copy (at most 512×512 pixels) without location or camera details, and uses it only to show on your profile.

Training data

  • Workouts, plans and programs you create or start, the sets, reps, weights and durations you log, the feedback you give after exercises, session history, and personal records derived from it.
  • Custom exercises you create (name, muscle groups, equipment, optional video link).

Purchase data

  • Subscription status (whether your account has an active LiftSmart Pro entitlement). Payment itself is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) — we never see your payment details.

Feedback

  • If you send feedback from the app, we receive the message you write and, for registered accounts, the email associated with your account so we can reply.

Usage and diagnostic data

  • App usage events — screens viewed and features used (for example that a workout was completed, how many sets it had and how long it took), app version, device model and an approximate location (country, region and city) worked out from your internet address. Mixpanel links them to your account identifier. See Analytics.
  • Screen recordings of how the app is used, with all text, photos and text fields blacked out on your phone before anything is sent. See Analytics.
  • Crash reports and performance data (for example that the app crashed, on which screen, with which app version and device model). These are linked to your account identifier so we can fix problems that affect you; they contain no training content.
  • On iOS, a device-integrity check (Apple App Attest) that confirms requests come from a genuine copy of the app. It sends no personal data.

Apart from the profile photo you choose to add, we do not collect photos. We also do not collect your precise location, contacts, health-app data or the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), and the app contains no advertising.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the service: store and sync your training data, build and adjust your program, show your history and records.
  • To personalize: match the catalog to your preferences, schedule and (if provided) gender.
  • To operate subscriptions: check whether your account is entitled to LiftSmart Pro.
  • To improve the app: understand which features are used and where people get stuck, through usage analytics and screen recordings with all text hidden, and find and fix crashes.
  • To respond when you contact us.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the performance of our contract with you (providing the app), your consent (optional data such as gender, and screen recordings, which start only after you continue past the notice when you first open the app), and our legitimate interest in improving and securing the service (usage analytics, which you can turn off at any time).

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that make the app work on our behalf: Google Firebase (sign-in, cloud data storage, crash reporting and Google Analytics), Mixpanel (usage analytics and screen recordings, stored in the European Union) and RevenueCat (subscription management). Apple or Google processes all payments, depending on where you subscribed, and if you sign in with Apple or Google, those providers handle the authentication. All data is transmitted and stored in encrypted form.

RevenueCat also passes subscription events (for example a started trial or a renewal) to Mixpanel, linked to your account identifier. This happens between their servers, so it continues even when Share usage data is turned off.

Google, Mixpanel and RevenueCat are US companies, so some data may be processed outside the European Union (Mixpanel keeps LiftSmart's data in the EU). These transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Mixpanel to understand how LiftSmart is used — for example which screens are opened, whether onboarding is finished, which exercises are swapped, and whether workouts are completed or left unfinished. The data contains no workout names, notes or other text you type.

  • Mixpanel links events to your account identifier (never your name or email) so we can understand how the app works for one person over time. Your analytics profile there also holds your onboarding answers (goal, experience, training preference and where you heard about us), your gender if you gave it, your subscription status and how many workouts you have completed. Mixpanel also records an approximate location (country, region and city) from your internet address, and stores all of this in the European Union.
  • Google Analytics uses a random app-instance identifier instead of your account identifier, and keeps its data for a limited period (at most 14 months).

Screen recordings

To see where the app is confusing, Mixpanel records what happens on screen while you use LiftSmart. All text, photos, images and text fields are blacked out on your phone before anything leaves it, so a recording shows the layout of the screens and where you tap — not what you wrote or who you are. Recordings start only after you continue past the notice when you first open the app, are uploaded only on Wi-Fi, and are kept for 30 days. Screens recorded while sharing was on may still be uploaded after you turn it off.

Your choice

You can turn usage analytics and screen recordings off at any time in Settings → Share usage data. Turning it off stops the collection and deletes your Mixpanel profile; events already sent are kept (in Mixpanel until you delete your account, in Google Analytics until they expire). We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), do not use analytics for advertising, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleting your account removes your profile and all training data from our servers, revokes the Sign in with Apple token where applicable, and deletes the authentication record. You can do this in the app under Profile → Delete account — see the step-by-step instructions.
  • Your profile photo is deleted from our servers when you remove or replace it in the app, and when you delete your account.
  • Abandoned guest accounts are cleaned up, including their data.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Feedback emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.
  • Deleting your account also asks Mixpanel to delete your usage data; this can take up to 30 days. Screen recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days. Google Analytics data is not linked to your account, so it is not deleted with it and expires on its own (after at most 14 months). If you keep an Apple subscription running after deleting your account, RevenueCat may still pass its renewal or cancellation events to Mixpanel.

6. Your rights

If you are in the European Union, the wider European Economic Area or the UK, the GDPR gives you these rights over your personal data:

  • Access — ask what we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correction — have wrong data fixed.
  • Deletion — delete your account and data yourself in the app (Profile → Delete account), or ask us.
  • Portability — receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection and restriction — object to processing based on our legitimate interest, or ask us to limit it.
  • Withdrawing consent — turn usage analytics and screen recordings off at any time in Settings → Share usage data. This does not affect what was collected before.

To use any of these rights, contact us; we reply within one month. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Lithuania, where LiftSmart is based, the State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt), or the authority in the country where you live. Wherever you live, if local law gives you similar rights, we will help with those too.

7. Children

LiftSmart is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, for significant changes, point it out in the app. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

LiftSmart, based in Lithuania, is responsible for your personal data (the data controller). For any privacy question or request, email liftsmartsupport@gmail.com.
