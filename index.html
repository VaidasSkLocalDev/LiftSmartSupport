Good questions

Before you start.A little clarity.

How is LiftSmart different from a workout tracker?

A tracker records what you did. LiftSmart also decides what you do next: it builds the program, sets weekly targets and adjusts them from your feedback. The tracker part is included and free.

Do I need experience to use it?

No. The questionnaire asks your level, and beginner plans start light and progress gradually. Every exercise has a video and step-by-step guidance.

Do I have to follow a program?

No. Quick workouts let you pick exercises and lift with no program at all, and everything still lands in your history.

What does Pro cost, and what is free?

Logging, history, personal records, the exercise library and up to four saved workouts are free forever. LiftSmart Pro unlocks Guided training and unlimited saved workouts. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency, monthly or yearly.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start as a guest. Sign in with Apple or Google keeps your data safe if you switch phones or reinstall.

Which devices does it support?

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Rest timers show as Live Activities on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. There are no ads, no data sales and no tracking across other apps. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
