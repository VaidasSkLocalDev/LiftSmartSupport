How the mesocycle engine works

Winging it leaves results on the table. Research on structured, periodized programs consistently shows greater strength gains than unstructured lifting — and autoregulating your load from feedback improves on fixed plans further. That's exactly what the engine automates.

Pick your starting point

Start a fresh block from one of 130+ templates, build your own, resume an active block — or continue a finished mesocycle and pick up just below your last peak instead of starting over.

Shape the block

Every week has an effort target measured in RIR — reps in reserve. You start comfortably at 3 in reserve, close in on your limit week by week, and finish with an easy recovery week. Pick how many weeks the block runs.

Make it yours

Lay out your training days, swap any exercise, tune rest times — then hit Start. Week one calibrates your working weights automatically; no 1RM tests, no math.

Train, rate, adapt

After each exercise you rate joint pain, pump and workload — seconds of input. Your answers shape next week's prescription: weights bumped, reps and set counts adjusted, deload scheduled. The guesswork is gone.
