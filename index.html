The mesocycle engine does the math

A mesocycle is a training block of several weeks. Pick your workouts once — LiftSmart handles the rest:

  • Week-one calibration learns your working weights
  • Weekly progression targets the right effort for each week
  • Volume adapts to your post-workout feedback
  • A deload week caps the block so you come back stronger
