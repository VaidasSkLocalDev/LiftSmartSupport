Delete Your Account

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data — workout history, plans, programs, personal records and profile — is removed from our servers. An active subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account; cancel it in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Delete from inside the app

  • Open LiftSmart and go to the Profile tab.
  • Scroll to the bottom of the profile screen.
  • Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your training data, profile and authentication record, and for Sign in with Apple accounts also revokes the Apple sign-in token.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, email us at liftsmartsupport@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

What about guest accounts?

Guest accounts follow the same in-app deletion path. If a guest account is abandoned (for example after switching to a registered account), it is cleaned up automatically together with its data.
